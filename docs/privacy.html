WORKIO PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 10, 2026

Summary: WORKIO is designed as an offline-first office work organizer. The current core version does not require an account, remote backend, Firebase, advertising, behavioral analytics, or cloud synchronization.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how WORKIO handles information when you use the WORKIO mobile application, package com.workio.office. WORKIO is intended to help users organize personal office activities such as tasks, meetings, quick notes, follow-ups, work sessions, and related productivity information.

2. Scope of this Policy

This Policy applies to the WORKIO mobile application and the informational pages provided for WORKIO. It describes the current core version of the app. If future versions introduce materially different data practices, this Policy should be updated before those features are released.

3. Information You Enter

Depending on how you use the app, you may enter task titles, task notes, due dates, priority levels, task status, meeting titles, meeting dates and times, names of people or teams, meeting notes, quick notes, follow-up titles, contact or client names typed by you, follow-up dates, work-session start and end times, and recorded break minutes.

4. Local Storage

The current core version stores its primary app data locally on your device using application storage, including technologies such as SharedPreferences. WORKIO does not require a remote server to perform its main features.

5. No Account Required

You do not need to create a WORKIO account, username, profile, or password to use the current core functionality.

6. No Firebase

The current core version is not designed to use Firebase for authentication, analytics, cloud databases, remote configuration, or other WORKIO data storage.

7. No Remote Backend

The current core version does not send your task, note, meeting, follow-up, or work-session records to a WORKIO-operated remote backend.

8. No Cloud Synchronization

The current core version does not provide WORKIO cloud sync between devices. Your locally stored data generally remains associated with the local app installation, subject to operating-system backup or restore behavior.

9. Tasks

Task information may include titles, notes, priority, status, creation time, and optional due dates. This information is used locally to display and organize your task list and productivity information.

10. Meetings

Meeting information may include a meeting title, date and time, person or team name entered by you, and optional notes. This information is used to display your meeting schedule inside the app.

11. Quick Notes

Quick Notes are text entries that you choose to save. They are intended for personal work organization and are stored locally in the current core version.

12. Follow-Ups

Follow-up records can include a subject, person or client name typed by you, date, and completion status. WORKIO uses this information to show pending and completed follow-ups.

13. Work Sessions

WORKIO can store work-session start times, end times, and break minutes entered or recorded through the app. These values are used to display work history and estimated focus-time information.

14. Work Insights

WORKIO may calculate values such as number of completed tasks, open tasks, overdue tasks, completion percentage, meeting count, completed follow-ups, and estimated focus time. These values are derived locally from information already stored in the app.

15. No Advertising

The current core version does not contain WORKIO-operated advertising and is not designed to use your app records for personalized advertising.

16. No Behavioral Analytics

The current core version is not designed to collect behavioral analytics about how you use WORKIO for advertising or profiling purposes.

17. No Cross-App Tracking

WORKIO is not designed to track your activity across unrelated third-party apps or websites for advertising purposes.

18. No Sale of Personal Information

WORKIO does not intentionally sell or rent the locally stored work records described in this Policy.

19. Device Permissions

The described core features do not require location, camera, microphone, contacts, or phone-call permissions. If a future version requests additional permissions, those permissions should be limited to the relevant feature and this Policy should be reviewed.

20. Location Information

The current core version does not need precise or approximate device location for its described functionality.

21. Camera and Photos

The current core version does not require camera access or photo-library access for its described functionality.

22. Microphone

The current core version does not require microphone access for its described functionality.

23. Contacts

WORKIO does not need access to your device contact list for the described core version. Any person, client, or team name shown in records is information you type yourself.

24. Payment Information

The current core functionality does not require you to enter payment-card or banking credentials into WORKIO.

25. Sensitive Business Information

Because the app can be used for office notes and work organization, you should avoid entering passwords, authentication codes, secret API keys, financial credentials, regulated records, highly confidential company secrets, or other information that should be stored in a specialized secure system.

26. Device Security

You are responsible for protecting access to your device. Consider using a device passcode, biometric lock, disk encryption, and other security features made available by your operating system.

27. Data Retention

Locally stored records generally remain on your device until you delete individual records, reset app data, clear app storage, uninstall the app, or the operating system removes the data.

28. Resetting App Data

WORKIO includes a Reset All Data option intended to remove app records such as tasks, meetings, notes, follow-ups, and work history from the current local app storage.

29. Uninstalling the App

Uninstalling WORKIO may remove locally stored data. However, device manufacturers and operating systems may provide backup or restore mechanisms outside WORKIO's direct control.

30. Device Backups

Your operating system or device provider may back up application data according to your device settings. Such backups are controlled by the operating-system or device platform rather than by WORKIO's core app logic.

31. Data Recovery

Because the current core version does not include WORKIO-operated cloud sync, we may not be able to recover local records after deletion, app removal, device loss, storage corruption, or a device reset.

32. Children

WORKIO is designed as a general productivity and office organization tool and is not specifically directed to young children. Users should follow applicable age requirements for the app store and their jurisdiction.

33. Third-Party Platforms

Distribution platforms such as Google Play may independently process technical, account, purchase, security, or diagnostic information under their own privacy terms. Those platform-level practices are separate from WORKIO's locally stored content.

34. This Website and GitHub Pages

The WORKIO informational pages may be hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub may process ordinary web-server information according to GitHub's own policies. These informational pages do not require you to submit your WORKIO task or office data.

35. Support Communications

If you contact support by email, your email provider and our email provider will process the information you voluntarily include in your message. Do not send passwords, payment credentials, or highly sensitive business content in support emails.

36. Legal Requirements

If legally required, information voluntarily provided to us through a support communication may be disclosed to comply with applicable law, legal process, or valid governmental requests. The current core app itself is designed without a WORKIO-operated server database of your local work records.

37. International Use

You may use WORKIO in different countries. Local app records remain on your device in the current core version, while third-party services such as app stores, email providers, or website hosting services may process their own data in other jurisdictions under their own terms.

38. Future Features

If future releases add user accounts, collaboration, remote backup, cloud synchronization, analytics, advertising, crash reporting, payments, attachments, notifications requiring new processing, or other online services, this Policy should be revised to accurately describe those practices.

39. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when WORKIO changes or when legal, technical, or app-store requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page identifies the current published version.

40. Your Choices

You choose what information to enter into WORKIO. You can avoid entering optional details, delete records using available app controls, reset all app data, or stop using the app.

41. No Professional Record-Keeping Guarantee

WORKIO is a personal organization tool. It should not be treated as the sole official repository for employment, compliance, legal, financial, medical, payroll, or other records that require specialized retention and security controls.
